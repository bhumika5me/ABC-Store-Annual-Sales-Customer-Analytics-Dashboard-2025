# ABC Store – Annual Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard 2025

## Project Overview

**ABC Store – Annual Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard 2025** is an Excel-based e-commerce sales and customer analytics project. The dashboard analyzes annual transaction data to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, order status, sales channels, geographic performance, age groups, gender, and B2B vs. B2C sales.

The project demonstrates foundational **Excel data analysis and dashboarding skills**, including formulas, PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers, data organization, KPI calculation, and business reporting.

## Objective

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze annual sales and revenue performance.
* Track orders, quantity sold, and average order value.
* Understand customer demographics such as age group and gender.
* Identify top-performing product categories.
* Compare sales performance across online channels.
* Analyze sales by geographic region/state.
* Monitor order and delivery status.
* Compare B2B and B2C sales.
* Present findings through an interactive Excel dashboard.

## Key Business Insights

1. **Total revenue is 204,323 from 50 orders**, with an average order value of approximately **4,086.46**.
2. **October is the strongest sales month**, contributing about 16.9% of annual revenue.
3. **November is the weakest sales month**, contributing about 4.0% of annual revenue.
4. **Male customers generate more revenue than female customers**, accounting for approximately 58.6% of total sales.
5. **Adults are the largest revenue-generating age group**, contributing approximately 64% of total revenue.
6. **Shoes are the top-performing product category**, generating 45,092 in revenue.
7. **Website is the largest sales channel by order count**, with 14 orders and 61,075 in revenue.
8. **Bagmati Province is the strongest geographic market**, generating 96,464 or approximately 47.2% of total revenue.
9. **B2C contributes slightly more total revenue than B2B**, while B2B has a higher average transaction value in this sample.
10. **Delivered orders account for 25 of the 50 transactions**, while the remaining transactions are distributed among shipped, pending, returned, and cancelled statuses.

## Data Quality Notes

The workbook contains 50 transaction records.

* No missing values were found in the main dataset.
* All 50 Order IDs are unique.
* All 50 Customer IDs are unique.
* The `Amount` field correctly matches `Qty × Current` for all records.
* Dates cover the full January–December 2025 period.
* Customer ages range from 21 to 55.

### Recommended Improvements

Before using the workbook as a final portfolio project, the following improvements are recommended:

* The `Project_Guide` uses **"In Transit"**, while the main `Status` field uses **"Shipped"**. These terms should be made consistent.
* The workbook contains both `Status` and `Ship-Status`; their meanings should be clearly distinguished.
* Rename the sheet `oder status` to **`order status`**.
* Configure the dashboard print/export area so the main dashboard fits cleanly onto one page.
* Add a clear currency label to the dashboard if the source amounts represent a specific currency.
